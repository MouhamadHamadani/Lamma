# Phase 15 content review

Every question that is NEW in Phase 15, **721 in all**, grouped by data file. The seeder only adds these; nothing that is already live was changed. A row is `difficulty | English question | correct answer (English)`; the Lebanon & Levant table adds the Arabic question and the Arabic correct answer, because that file is the one that needs a native read.

## Where a native-Arabic and a Lebanese read matters most

1. **`lebanon-levant.php`, the dialect questions** (marked `dialect` in the table below). The Arabic is the Lebanese dialect on purpose; the wording, the spelling of the dialect words (e.g. «هلّأ», «كتير», «قدّيش», «لسّا», «كرمال», «شو في ما في») and the English meaning given with each need a Lebanese speaker's ear. The options of those questions mix dialect and plain Arabic.
2. **Food and drink names** in `lebanon-levant.php` (manousheh, kaak, maamoul, shanklish, sfouf, znoud el-Sit, layali Lubnan, jallab, sahlab, mouneh, hindbeh, kishk, kibbeh nayyeh, muhammara) and in `food-drink.php` (koshari, qamar al-Din, luqaimat, basbousa, umm Ali, maqluba): spelling in both scripts, and that the description in the question is the one people use at home.
3. **Place and people names**: Lebanese towns, rivers and sites (Bsharri, Berdawni, Jezzine, Batroun, Nahr el-Kalb, Qurnat as Sawda, Qadisha, Shouf, Anjar), the Rahbani, Gibran, Maalouf, Choucair and Saab entries, and the Levantine month names in `words-language.php` (Hazeiran, Tammuz, Aylul, Nisan, Adhar, Ayyar, Kanun, Tishrin).
4. **Sayings**: the dialect sayings (`يعطيك العافية`, `صحتين`, `شو في ما في`) and the «لقب» nicknames (جارة القمر, أسود الأطلس, ثعالب الصحراء, نسور قرطاج).
5. **Arabic transliterations of foreign names** across all files (players, composers, painters, brands): the Arabic spelling of each, e.g. «ديكيمبي موتومبو», «ريمسكي كورساكوف», «بوتيتشيلي».

Everything else is Modern Standard Arabic and a normal proofread is enough.
