How it works

Game night in three steps

1

Create a room

Pick categories, number of questions, timer and difficulty. You get a short room code to share.

2

Friends join

They open the site on their phones, type the code, choose a language and tap Ready.

3

Play and climb

Answer before the timer runs out. Points after every question, and a podium at the end.
